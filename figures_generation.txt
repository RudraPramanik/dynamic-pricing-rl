Figure Generation Procedure
===========================
Project: Econometric-Guided Deep Q-Network (EG-DQN) dynamic pricing paper
Source notebook: Dynamic_pricing_hybrid_EG_DQN.ipynb
Manuscript figures: figures/fig-<label>.png (matched to LaTeX \label{fig:...})

This document describes how every figure used in the paper was produced.
All seven figures come from a single sequential run of the experimental
notebook. They were originally written to temporary paths under /tmp/ and
later copied into figures/ with publication names.


1. Software stack
-----------------
- Python (NumPy, pandas)
- statsmodels (Poisson GLM via formula API)
- matplotlib + seaborn (plotting)
- scipy (distributions / significance tests elsewhere in the notebook)
- NumPy-based Deep Q-Network (no PyTorch / TensorFlow dependency)

Plot theme:
  sns.set_theme(style="whitegrid", palette="husl", font_scale=1.1)

Export settings (all figures):
  plt.savefig(..., dpi=120, bbox_inches='tight')


2. Reproducibility settings
---------------------------
SEED = 42
  np.random.seed(SEED)
  random.seed(SEED)

Simulation horizon:
  N_DAYS       = 365 * 4   # 1,460 daily observations
  START_DATE   = 2021-01-01
  HOLDOUT_DAYS = 120       # final 120 days = test / holdout
  TRAIN_DAYS   = N_DAYS - HOLDOUT_DAYS

Products: item_1, item_2, item_3 (substitutes)
Focus of RL / revenue / forecast figures: Item 1
Item 1 discrete action grid: 20 prices on [$6.0, $16.0]


3. End-to-end pipeline (run order)
----------------------------------
Figures are not standalone scripts. Each plot depends on objects created
earlier in the notebook. The required order is:

  (1) Imports, seed, plot style
  (2) Ground-truth DGP parameters
  (3) Synthetic price + demand generation
  (4) Exploratory Data Analysis          --> fig-eda
  (5) Feature engineering
  (6) Own/cross-price GLM elasticity fit --> fig-elasticity_validation
  (7) Full multivariate GLM forecast     --> fig-forecast
  (8) GLM-optimal price signal (EG-DQN anchor)
  (9) PricingEnv + NumpyDQN definitions
 (10) Train Pure DQN and EG-DQN (300 episodes)
 (11) Convergence plots                  --> fig-convergence
 (12) Evaluate five pricing strategies on holdout
 (13) Revenue comparison panels          --> fig-revenue
 (14) Ablation study (200 episodes)      --> fig-ablation
 (15) Rolling 12-month GLM elasticity    --> fig-rolling_elasticity

To regenerate all figures: run the notebook top-to-bottom with SEED=42,
then rename the /tmp exports to the manuscript filenames below.

5. Per-figure generation details
--------------------------------

5.1 fig-eda.png  (label: fig:eda)
  Notebook section: Exploratory Data Analysis
  Layout: figure 20 x 14 in; 3 products x 3 panels
  Panels per product:
    - Left: daily demand (solid) and list price (dashed, twin axis)
    - Centre: log(price) vs log(demand) scatter with OLS slope;
              true own-price elasticity annotated
    - Right: demand histogram with normal density overlay (mu, sigma)
  Data: full synthetic series (2021--2024), M5/Favorita-calibrated DGP
  Title in notebook: "Exploratory Data Analysis — Retail Demand Simulation"

5.2 fig-elasticity_validation.png  (label: fig:elasticity_validation)
  Notebook section: GLM elasticity validation plot
  Layout: 1 x 3 subplots, figsize (18, 5)
  Per product (train window only):
    - Scatter of price vs demand
    - GLM fitted demand curve using estimated own-price elasticity
    - Ground-truth demand curve using simulator elasticity
    - Relative bias annotation
  Model: Poisson log-link GLM fit on training days only
  Title: "GLM Elasticity Estimation vs Ground Truth"

5.3 fig-forecast.png  (label: fig:forecast)
  Notebook section: GLM demand forecast visualization (Item 1)
  Layout: 2 x 1, figsize (16, 10)
  Panel A: training actual vs fitted; holdout actual vs forecast;
           vertical train/test split line
  Panel B: test residuals (actual − forecast) with mean and ±1 std band
  Model: full multivariate Poisson GLM (own/cross prices, calendar,
         lags / rolling features as engineered in the notebook)
  Title: "GLM Demand Forecast — item_1 (Poisson log-link)"

5.4 fig-convergence.png  (label: fig:convergence)
  Notebook section: DQN training convergence
  Layout: 1 x 3, figsize (18, 5)
  Upstream: Pure DQN and EG-DQN each trained for EPISODES = 300
            (hidden_dim=128, NumPy DQN + replay buffer)
  Panel A: episode revenue (raw translucent + 20-episode moving average);
           heuristic "convergence" marker when MA exceeds 80% of final MA
  Panel B: mean squared TD-error (log scale), 20-episode MA
  Panel C: epsilon exploration decay (epsilon_min reference at 0.05)
  Title: "DQN Training Convergence — Pure DQN vs EG-DQN"
  Note: the manuscript treats the centre (TD-error) panel as exported
        from the notebook and does not over-interpret it as a formal
        convergence proof.

5.5 fig-revenue.png  (label: fig:revenue)
  Notebook section: strategy comparison on the Item 1 holdout
  Layout: 2 x 2, figsize (18, 12)
  Upstream: five policies evaluated on the last HOLDOUT_DAYS = 120 days:
            Fixed-Price, Rule-Based, GLM-Only, Pure DQN, EG-DQN
  Panel A: cumulative revenue over test days
  Panel B: daily revenue boxplots (notched)
  Panel C: price trajectories vs reference price
  Panel D: total holdout revenue bars (authoritative totals for Table)
  Title: "Strategy Comparison — Revenue & Pricing Analysis (Test Set)"

5.6 fig-ablation.png  (label: fig:ablation)
  Notebook section: ablation study visualization
  Layout: 1 x 2, figsize (16, 6)
  Upstream: ABLATION_EPISODES = 200 per configuration, SEED reset each time
  Configurations:
    - Pure DQN   (no GLM reward shaping)
    - DQN+Reward (GLM reward shaping, lambda_glm = 30)
    - EG-DQN     (full hybrid as configured in that ablation cell)
  Panel A: training reward curves (15-episode moving average)
  Panel B: holdout/test total revenue bars with %-gain vs Pure DQN
  Title: "Ablation Study — GLM Component Contributions"

5.7 fig-rolling_elasticity.png  (label: fig:rolling_elasticity)
  Notebook section: rolling GLM elasticity stability (Item 1)
  Layout: single axes, figsize (14, 5)
  Estimation:
    - Rolling window = 365 days (12 months)
    - Step = 30 days
    - Windows stop before the holdout (no test leakage)
    - Poisson GLM; own-price coefficient ± 1.96 * SE band
  Overlay: horizontal line at true own-price elasticity
  Title: "Rolling 12-Month GLM Own-Price Elasticity Estimate — item_1"


6. Synthetic data used by the plots
-----------------------------------
Prices: discrete step-function paths (n_changes=60), product-specific
        reference prices and ranges from GROUND_TRUTH.
Demand: multiplicative log-linear DGP with base level, trend, annual and
        weekend seasonality, own- and cross-price elasticities, Gaussian
        noise; clipped to a minimum of 1 unit.
Calibration intent: statistical properties inspired by M5 / Favorita-style
retail series (explicit parameters logged in the notebook for validation).


7. How to regenerate for journal submission
-------------------------------------------
1. Open Dynamic_pricing_hybrid_EG_DQN.ipynb.
2. Ensure dependencies are installed:
     numpy pandas statsmodels matplotlib seaborn scipy
3. Run all cells in order (do not skip training cells).
4. Confirm SEED remains 42 and episode counts remain 300 / 200.
5. Collect the seven PNGs from /tmp/ (or change savefig paths to figures/).
6. Rename to fig-<label>.png as in Section 4.
7. Recompile the LaTeX manuscript (index.tex) so includegraphics paths resolve.

Optional for camera-ready: re-export at higher dpi (e.g. 300) by changing
dpi=120 in each plt.savefig call; layout code is otherwise unchanged.


8. Authority note
-----------------
The figures/ copies used in the paper are the authoritative visual record
of the reported research run. Numeric claims in the manuscript that cite
a figure (especially fig-revenue Panel D) take those plotted results as
source of truth for that run.
